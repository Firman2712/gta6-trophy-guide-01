About this guide

Names and descriptions are the official PlayStation text, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026. Every guide on this page is written in our own words for this entry and names its sources beside it — a specific location or step only counts here when it cites where it came from. Where no reliable method was known on launch day (19 November 2026), the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.

There are no online trophies: all 55 are single-player. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep trophy runs cheat-free. This app works fully offline: it loads nothing from the network, and your progress never leaves your browser unless you export it.
