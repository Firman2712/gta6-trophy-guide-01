Newly confirmed — 4 trophies

PowerPyx has now pinned down four trophies that were unknown at launch: In The Beginning… (Ocean Beach cocktail), Capital Offense (blue Banshee through the showroom window), The Truth Is Out There (the UFO easter egg), and Ten Pin (bike melee drive-by). Each carries a Newly confirmed badge in the list.

One trophy can be missed — protect it in Chapter 1

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as possibly missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning. Follow her to the balcony and accept — after Chapter 1 that moment is gone. Everything else on this list can be finished after the story.

The road to the platinum — 4 stages

Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx’s roadmap. Difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no mission replay — so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving: keep them off.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, guard the meditation

    60–80 hours · Chapters 1–5

    Finish the story and enjoy it. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation with Lucia, scan vehicles and study animals as you go, and take every optional mission you pass. Progress toward the 100% checklist already counts.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist & 70 of 80 challenges

    ~100 hours · after the story

    The checklist opens under Progress, then Stats. It is lenient — about 60% true completion — and needs every robbery, underwater find, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest, plus 70 of 80 challenges, so you can skip the hardest ten.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and side cleanup

    ~10 hours · free-roam

    Strangers are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 counting strands and the character questlines that are still open — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — plus any side trophy left behind.

  4. Stage 4 — Money, garages, and the last sweeps

    Cleanup · free-roam

    Launder $250,000 of marked bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy the 8 garages, register 10 vehicles, and mop up the remaining activities and collectibles. The platinum pops with the last trophy.
