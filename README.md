An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: a checklist with progress saved in the browser, a cited guide for every trophy, a four-stage roadmap to the platinum, missable-trophy warnings, and a spoiler-free mode.

## What changed in round 2 (20 Nov 2026)

Four trophies that were unconfirmed on launch day now have real, cited methods, confirmed by PowerPyx on 20 Nov 2026: **In The Beginning…** (Apple Martini at the Ocean View Rooftop Bar, drunk standing next to the bar), **Capital Offense** (steal the blue Banshee from the south Vice City showroom with the Key Cloner from Chapter 3's "Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs", then drive through the window), **Ten Pin** (kick 10 pedestrians with Square from any bike within 60 seconds) and **The Truth Is Out There** (UFO Easter Egg north-west of the Starlet Motel in Kelly County, after 2AM, solo — follow the blinking car into the woods; corroborated by GameRant).

The app now marks those four with a gold "Newly confirmed · 20 Nov 2026" badge, a filter chip that shows only them, a gold banner at the top of the page, and a star in the roadmap chips. Open the filtered view directly with `?filter=new`. Four more guides were expanded with confirmed detail: **Fair Game** (Bret's chain opens after Chapter 2's Coffee Break, Jason-only, first quest Veteran Hunted in Lummox County, Killtelligence SM-69 rifle, 15 Target Decks), **Let's Go Bowling** (all 3 Kingpin Bowling valuables: luxury watch, Gutterball Moreland 850 shotgun, safe blown with Sticky Bombs; trophy pops after escaping police; not missable), **Personal Best** (check Strength on the touchpad; train together for double progress; fitness clubs via Map > Activities) and **Actions Speak Louder** (view your count in the trophy list; turn on Criminal Profile HUD notifications). **Positive Mental Attitude** gained a supporting note: the Chapter 1 meditation adds +1 in the Progress menu even though it is not labelled a Meditation Spot, and PowerPyx's author earned the trophy having done it. **Bad Luck, I Guess** is still unconfirmed, but now carries PowerPyx's lead: believed to relate to the Payday Loans store from the Prologue, in Vice City — Little Cuba. All 55 guides and steps were rewritten in fresh wording for this round.
